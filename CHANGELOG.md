# Changelog

Every version of Playbook By Sovereign and what it changed.

## 0.1.0 (in progress)

The first version.

- **Planner.** Ask for the best comp for a map, side, site and rank, for a five-stack, trio or duo. Fix any part (agents that must be in, agents left out, teammates already locked in, an exact number of a role) and leave the rest open, or ask for the best map, side or rank instead.
- **Answers you can read.** Every comp comes with a fit from 0 to 100, its strengths and gaps for the situation, the pairs that help or clash, and how it compares with what pro teams play.
- **Agents and Maps.** Every agent's kit in the game's own words, and every map's sites and callouts on its minimap.
- **Parties.** Save your usual duo, trio or five-stack with the agents each of you plays, and plan for it in one click.
- **Hush**, the newest Duelist, is in with early estimates that will be updated once there's play to go on.
