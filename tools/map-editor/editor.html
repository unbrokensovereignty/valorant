Maps

How it works

  • Move a callout or mark by dragging its dot, its name or the mark itself. Click one to select it, then nudge it with the arrow keys (hold Shift for bigger steps) or type its place on the right.
  • Add a callout or a mark: pick it in the tools (or press its key), then click the map. Name it or describe it on the right. Teleporters and ziplines get two ends; a rope can have a second end too.
  • Remove what's selected with Delete or the button on the right. Anything removed can be brought back from the list.
  • Zoom with the mouse wheel, the buttons or + and −; drag an empty spot to move around, and 0 shows the whole map again.
  • Floor shows in green where the app reads the floor. Every callout and mark has to stand on it, or the app's own checks fail; anything in a wall gets an orange ring, and the right side offers to move it onto the floor.
  • Names sit next to their dot the way the app places them (above, or below, right or left when another name is in the way), so put each dot in the middle of the area it names.
  • Your work is kept in this browser as you go, so you can close the file and come back. When you're done, choose Save edits file and send that file over. Open edits file loads one back in to keep going.
  • Save picture saves the map you're on as an image, with its names and marks.

Ctrl+Z undoes and Ctrl+Y redoes; Esc goes back to moving things.
